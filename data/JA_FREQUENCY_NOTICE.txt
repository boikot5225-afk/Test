data/ja_vocab_frequency.tsv is derived from two sources.

Word order comes from wordfreq (large_ja), by Robyn Speer, distributed under the
Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
Its Japanese data aggregates several public corpora; the package documents the
list of sources it combines.

The vocabulary itself — which tokens count as words, their dictionary forms,
readings and parts of speech — comes from JMdict, the property of the Electronic
Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), used in conformance with the
Group's licence:
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
JMdict is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0), which this derived file inherits.

Rebuild with scripts/make_ja_freq.py, which reads the wordfreq package and
data/ja_dict_core.json.
